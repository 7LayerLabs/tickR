## FAQ (Accompanying This Page)

**Q: Is this real money trading?**
A: No. TickR uses simulated cash only. Your child can't lose real money, and you're never charged for their trading.

**Q: How do I know this is safe?**
A: We use real market data and real companies, but everything is simulated. Our moderation is strict, and parents have full visibility.

**Q: Will my kid get addicted to trading?**
A: We designed it to discourage short-term speculation. Points are awarded daily, not intraday. Learning is prioritized over winning. Competition is opt-in.

**Q: What if my kid loses money (simulated)?**
A: That's the point. They'll learn how to manage losses without real consequences. Parents see their portfolio, so you can discuss what happened.

**Q: How much time will this take?**
A: 30 minutes a week is plenty. It's not a time sink. It's designed for healthy engagement.

**Q: When can my kid enter tournaments?**
A: Only after completing Level 7, which typically takes 4–8 weeks of casual play. This protects beginners and rewards dedication.
