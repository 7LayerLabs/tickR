# Pricing Strategy (FINAL)

**This price is right. Don't touch it.**

## Price Points

### Free Tier
**$0/month (Unlimited)**

**What's Included:**
- Starter sectors (4 total):
  - Tech
  - Fashion & Lifestyle
  - Entertainment & Gaming
  - Everyday Brands
- Limited stocks per sector (20–30 most popular)
- Solo portfolio only
- Learning Levels 1–3
- Basic portfolio tracking

**Best For:**
- Testing if the product is for them
- Casual learners
- Kids without parental buy-in yet

---

### Paid Tier
**$5.99/month OR $49/year (20% savings)**

**What's Included:**
- All sectors (unlocked as they level up)
- All stocks (entire stock universe)
- Games (create/join private games)
- Leaderboards (global + friends)
- Community board (moderated discussion)
- Parent dashboard (full visibility)
- Learning Levels 4–7
- Advanced metrics (P/E, PEG, growth %)
- Badges & achievements

**Best For:**
- Serious learners
- Kids in families with parental oversight
- Kids interested in competitions

---

## Pricing Model Rationale

### Why $5.99/month?

1. **Affordable for kids/parents**
   - Below typical app subscription ($9.99+)
   - Below Spotify/Netflix tier
   - Not a major family decision

2. **Justifiable value**
   - Full stock data costs money
   - Moderation/community support costs
   - Parent dashboard is premium feature

3. **Psychological**
   - $5.99 feels "reasonable," not "expensive"
   - $49/year feels like a deal (good for commitment)

4. **Not a barrier**
   - If a kid really wants it, parents will pay
   - If unsure, free tier is enough to learn basics

### Why $49/year?

- 20% discount incentivizes annual commitment
- Parent-friendly: "That's less than Netflix"
- Reduces churn from monthly cancellations

## Freemium Strategy

### Free-to-Paid Conversion Flow

1. **Kid starts free**
   - Plays with 4 sectors
   - Completes Levels 1–3
   - Hits games/leaderboard paywall → prompted to upgrade

2. **Parent sees value**
   - Child learning real concepts
   - Safe, moderated environment
   - Parent dashboard shows progress

3. **Convert at natural moment**
   - "Want to compete with friends?" → Games upgrade
   - "Ready for advanced stock metrics?" → Unlock P/E
   - "Join global leaderboard?" → Leaderboard upgrade

### Conversion Targets

- **Target 10–15% free-to-paid conversion** within 30 days
- **Expected ARPU (Annual Revenue Per User):** $6–8

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Aggressive paywalls (kids hate it)
❌ Dark patterns (misleading upgrade prompts)
❌ Free trial shenanigans (auto-charge)
❌ Multiple pricing tiers (too confusing)
❌ Game pass model (too complex for kids)

---

## Payment Processing

- Stripe for payments
- Parent email verification required
- Monthly billing default
- Annual billing highlighted as "better deal"
- Easy cancellation (no phone call required)

---

## Refund Policy

**Simple:**
- 30-day money-back guarantee
- No questions asked
- Refund to original payment method

**Why?** Parents need to feel safe. If they're not happy, they leave. No fighting over refunds.

## Financial Model (Rough)

**Assumptions:**
- 10k users at launch
- 12% conversion to paid
- 1,200 paid users × $5.99 = $7,188/month
- $49 annual tier @ 30% of paid = ~$18k/year additional

**Math:**
- Conservative first year: ~$100k–150k ARR
- Year 2 (with growth): $500k–1M ARR potential

**Cost Structure:**
- Stock data API: ~$500–2k/month (depends on volume)
- Hosting: $2–5k/month
- Moderation (1 person): $3–4k/month
- Ops/support: $2–3k/month

**Gross margin:** 70%+ (software business)

---

## Pricing Lock-In

**Do not change pricing after launch.** Once users pay, keep the price same for them (grandfather existing customers).

Only new users pay adjusted prices.

This builds trust.
