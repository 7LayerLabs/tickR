# How TickR Works (Explainer Page)

## Main Headline
TickR is simple to start — and deep enough to grow with your child.

---

## Step 1: Start With Play Money

Every player begins with **$10,000 in simulated cash**.

No real money. No risk.

---

## Step 2: Buy Real Stocks

Kids can buy shares of real companies like Nike, Apple, Netflix, Roblox, and more — using **real market prices**.

They'll see:
- How many shares they own
- Portfolio value
- Daily gains and losses

---

## Step 3: Learn While Playing

When stocks move, TickR explains why.

Through short challenges and quizzes, kids learn:
- Why prices change
- How earnings, trends, and competition matter
- How different sectors behave

**Lessons unlock naturally as kids progress.**

---

## Step 4: Earn Points to Unlock More

Points are earned by:
- Completing lessons
- Answering quizzes
- Managing portfolios responsibly

Points unlock:
- New sectors
- Advanced metrics
- Competitive modes

**Points are never used as money.**

---

## Step 5: Compete (When Ready)

Kids can:
- Play solo
- Create private games with friends
- Qualify for advanced tournaments

**Competition is structured to reward:**
- Strategy
- Consistency
- Learning — not luck

---

## Step 6: Track Progress Over Time

Parents and kids can see:
- Growth in knowledge
- Smarter decision-making
- Improved risk awareness

**TickR grows with the player.**

---

## What TickR Is (and Isn't)

**✔ Educational**
**✔ Skill-building**
**✔ Real-world relevant**

**❌ Gambling**
**❌ Real trading**
**❌ Get-rich-quick schemes**

**TickR teaches kids how investing works — not how to chase hype.**
