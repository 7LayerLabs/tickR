## Tone Guide

- **Encouraging** (not patronizing)
- **Clear** (no financial jargon)
- **Casual** (like a friend, not a bank)
- **Honest** (acknowledge losses matter)

### What NOT to Say

❌ "Maximize your returns"
❌ "Beat the market"
❌ "Get rich"
❌ "Invest wisely"
❌ "Passive income"

### What TO Say

✔ "Learn how stocks work"
✔ "See what you can build"
✔ "Manage your portfolio"
✔ "Make smarter decisions"
✔ "Level up your knowledge"

---

## Emoji Usage

Used sparingly and intentionally:
- 🎮 = Game/fun
- 📈 = Growth/levels
- 🎉 = Achievement
- ✓ = Correct/success
- ❌ = Error/needs action
- 💡 = Tips/learning
- 🏆 = Competition
- 💰 = Money/portfolio

Not used:
- Unnecessary decoration
- Multiple emoji per button
- Emoji in headlines (except achievement screens)
