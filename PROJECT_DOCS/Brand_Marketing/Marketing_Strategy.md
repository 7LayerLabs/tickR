# Marketing Strategy (What to Tell Marketing)

---

## A. YOUR MESSAGING PILLARS (Repeat These Everywhere)

### The 5 Core Messages

1. **Real market, safe environment**
   - Real prices, no real money
   - Kids experience real market behavior without real risk

2. **Competition with discipline**
   - Composite scoring (performance + diversification + consistency + learning)
   - Earned tournaments (qualify through learning, not luck)
   - Reckless behavior can't win

3. **Built for 12–16**
   - Not adults, not little kids
   - Age-appropriate progression
   - Designed for the real interests of Gen Z

4. **Parents stay in control**
   - Full dashboard + visibility
   - Moderated community (no DMs)
   - Opt-in leaderboards

5. **Learn skills, not shortcuts**
   - No "get rich" framing
   - No hype language
   - Focus on long-term thinking, emotional discipline, diversification

---

## B. THE WORDS YOU MUST NOT USE (SERIOUSLY)

### Banned Words/Phrases

❌ "Make money" / "Earn returns" / "Beat the market"
❌ "Get rich" / "Profit"
❌ "Predict" / "Signals" / "Hot picks"
❌ "YOLO" / "Moon" / "Lambo"
❌ "Winning" (in a money context)

**Why?** These sound like you're selling a finance scam to minors. You're not.

### Use These Instead

✔ "Learn" / "Practice" / "Build skills"
✔ "Understand" / "Train" / "Master"
✔ "Real companies, real prices"
✔ "Structured competition"
✔ "Portfolio strategy" / "Risk management"
✔ "Disciplined investing"

   - "How TickR prevents bad investing habits"

   - **Goal:** Justify parent purchase

   - "Best reflections from the community"

## F. CONVERSION STRATEGY (Free → Paid)

### The Free-to-Paid Journey

**Day 0–2: Activation**
- Sign up (free)
- Build portfolio (free)
- See first green/red day (free)
- Complete first lesson (free)

**Day 3–7: Engagement**
- Invite friend to game (show paywall: games are paid feature)
- Check portfolio daily (free)
- Complete Level 2 (free)
- See P/E ratio (locked: "Unlock with $5.99/month")

**Week 2: Upsell Moment**
- Friend accepts game invite → "You need paid TickR to play"
- OR parent sees dashboard feature → "Get full parent visibility for $5.99/month"
- OR leaderboard visible but stats hidden → "See full leaderboards when you upgrade"

**Value Prop at Upsell**
- Games (compete with friends, real-time scoring)
- Leaderboards (see how you rank)
- Community (discuss with other kids)
- Parent dashboard (parents stay involved)
- All sectors unlocked (not just starter 4)
- Advanced metrics (P/E, PEG, growth %)

### Conversion Targets

- Free tier: 85%+ of signups
- Paid conversion: 12–15% within 30 days
- MRR per 10k users: $5,988 (10k × 0.12 × $5.99)

### Churn Reduction

- Email parents with their child's progress (weekly)
- Celebrate milestones (level-ups, achievements)
- New content weekly (keeps it fresh)
- Parent success story emails (testimonials)

   - Landing page: Parents page
   - Budget: Start $1k/week

3. **TikTok / YouTube Ads** (Brand awareness)
   - Target: Ages 12–20
   - Format: 15-sec video (brand-focused hooks)

   - Budget: Start $500/week

4. **Reddit Sponsorships** (Niche communities)
   - r/personalfinance, r/investing, r/parenting
   - Native sponsorship (not ads)
   - Budget: $500–2k per month

## K. BRAND VOICE & TONE

### Sound Like

- Confident (know what we're doing)
- Friendly (approachable, not stuffy)
- Honest (acknowledge risks, don't oversell)
- Empowering (kids can do this, parents should enable it)

### Don't Sound Like

- Pushy (don't hard-sell)
- Condescending (don't talk down)
- Corporate (don't use finance jargon)
- Hype-y (don't sound like a scam)

### Example Headlines

✔ "Learn how stocks work. Before you risk real money."
✔ "Invest like an adult. Think like a student."
✔ "Competition that teaches discipline."
✔ "Financial literacy that doesn't feel like school."

❌ "Get rich quick with real stocks!"
❌ "Beat the market before age 16"
❌ "Your kids will be millionaires"
