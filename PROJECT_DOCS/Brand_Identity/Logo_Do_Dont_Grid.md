# TickR Logo Do / Don't Grid (For Quick Reference)

## DO

* Use official logo files only
* Keep logo clean and uncluttered
* Use mascot-only at small sizes
* Respect spacing and contrast
* Place logo in calm, structured environments
* Let the arrow clearly signal progress

---

## DON'T

* Recolor the logo
* Add gradients or neon effects
* Animate logo with hype or "to-the-moon" motion
* Meme the mascot
* Place logo near gambling, crypto, or hype language
* Modify typography or proportions
* Create "just for fun" logo versions

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

* Using the mascot as a joke character
* Removing the arrow for "simplicity"
* Making the logo louder to feel more "gamey"
* Overusing the full logo when mascot-only is cleaner

**Discipline > Decoration. Always.**
