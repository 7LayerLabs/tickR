# TickR Mascot Personality & Expression Guide

This is the guardrail that keeps TickR from drifting into cringe.

## Mascot Role

The TickR mascot represents:

* Learning through experience
* Calm confidence
* Progress over hype
* Discipline over luck

He is **not** a cheerleader.
He is **not** a clown.
He is **not** a hype machine.

Think: *coach*, not *influencer*.

---

## Core Personality Traits

* Curious
* Focused
* Encouraging
* Calm under pressure
* Observant, not reactive

---

## Approved Expressions (In-App Only)

These can be designed as **separate assets**, not logo variants:

* Neutral / Default (logo expression)
* Thinking / Analyzing
* Learning / Progressing
* Small win acknowledgment (subtle, not celebratory)

---

## Expressions That Are NOT Allowed

* Overexcitement
* Greed or money fixation
* Shock or panic
* Mocking or sarcasm
* "Winning big" reactions

If it looks like a slot machine character, it's wrong.

---

## Mascot Usage Rules

* Logo usage = neutral, focused expression only
* In-app usage = expressions tied to learning moments
* Never use mascot to imply:
  * financial gains
  * beating the market
  * fast money
  * superiority over others

---

## The Arrow Rule (Sacred)

The arrow:

* Always points upward
* Always represents progress, not profit
* Is never stylized into hype shapes
* Is never removed

If the arrow goes, the meaning breaks.

---

## Final Mascot Test

Ask:

> "Would a parent trust this character teaching their kid?"

If the answer is anything but yes, revise.

---

## Final Lock Statement (Use This Internally)

> "TickR's logo and mascot are tools of trust. We protect them by prioritizing clarity, discipline, and long-term credibility over short-term attention."
