## 🎨 VISUAL IDENTITY SUMMARY

**See [Logo Usage Guidelines v1.0](./Logo_Usage_Guidelines_v1.0.md) for the official locked specification.**

### Logo System
- **Primary:** Mascot + Wordmark (Mascot + TickR)
- **Mascot Mark:** Robot + Arrow (Iconic symbol)
- **Wordmark:** "TickR" text (Serious/limited space)
- **Principle:** Discipline over hype. Arrow = Progress.

### Tactical Guides
- [Designer Handoff Checklist](./Designer_Handoff_Checklist.md)
- [Logo Do / Don't Grid](./Logo_Do_Dont_Grid.md)
- [Mascot Personality & Expression Guide](./Mascot_Personality_and_Expression_Guide.md)

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep teal or slate blue (trust, intelligence, stability)
- **Accent:** Soft green (growth, achievement, positive)
- **Neutrals:** White, charcoal, light gray (clarity, readability)

### Typography
- **Headline:** Modern, rounded, confident (not corporate, not playful)
- **Body:** Clear, readable, accessible (no jargon)

### Logo Applications

| Context | What to Use |
|---------|------------|
| Website headers, marketing pages | Primary Logo (Mascot + Wordmark) |
| App store, pitch decks | Primary Logo (Mascot + Wordmark) |
| App icon, favicon, loading screens | Mascot-Only Mark (Robot + Arrow) |
| Avatars, badges | Mascot-Only Mark (Robot + Arrow) |
| Documents, school materials | Wordmark-Only |
| Parent-facing PDFs | Wordmark-Only |
| Serious tone / limited space | Wordmark-Only |

## 📱 BRAND TOUCHPOINTS & GUIDELINES

### Landing Page & Marketing
- **Logo Use:** Wordmark + optional icon
- **Mascot:** Introduced later, in context
- **Tone:** Professional, trust-focused
- **Color:** Full palette, with emphasis on primary color
- **Message:** Safety, learning, structure

### App Experience
- **Logo Use:** Small, unobtrusive (top-left or header)
- **Mascot:** Central and expressive (animations, personality)
- **Tone:** Warm, encouraging, approachable
- **Color:** Full palette, with green accents on achievements
- **Message:** Learning progress, understanding, growth

### Investor Materials
- **Logo Use:** Wordmark only, professional
- **Mascot:** Mention only, not visual
- **Tone:** Precise, data-driven, strategic
- **Color:** B&W or primary color (no gradients)
- **Message:** System design, defensibility, discipline

### Legal & Trust Pages
- **Logo Use:** Wordmark only
- **Mascot:** Not used
- **Tone:** Formal, clear, transparent
- **Color:** Primary color or black
- **Message:** Safety, compliance, credibility

### Email Communications
- **Logo Use:** Wordmark in header (professional)
- **Mascot:** Not used in parent emails; optional in user emails
- **Tone:** Personalized but professional
- **Color:** Primary color in header, neutral in body
- **Message:** Update, progress, value

### Social Media (B2C)
- **Logo Use:** Wordmark or icon
- **Mascot:** Can be featured (animated, personality-forward)
- **Tone:** Friendly, encouraging, educational
- **Color:** Full palette
- **Message:** Learning moments, progress, community

### Social Media (B2B/Educator)
- **Logo Use:** Wordmark only
- **Mascot:** Not used
- **Tone:** Professional, authoritative
- **Color:** Primary color
- **Message:** Curriculum, outcomes, partnership

## 💬 BRAND VOICE & TONE GUIDELINES

### Always Sound Like
- A mentor (helpful, not superior)
- A guide (accompanying, not leading blindly)
- A teacher (explaining, not preaching)
- A friend (warm, not formal)
- An expert (confident, not arrogant)

### Never Sound Like
- A salesperson (pushy, always selling)
- A corporate entity (stiff, jargon-heavy)
- A condescender (too simple, patronizing)
- A hype-y app (excited about everything)
- A know-it-all (certain about unknowns)

### Message Examples

❌ **Wrong:** "TickR will make you a better investor!"
✅ **Right:** "TickR helps you understand how markets work."

❌ **Wrong:** "Buy this stock now!"
✅ **Right:** "Here's why the price moved today."

❌ **Wrong:** "You're crushing it!"
✅ **Right:** "You're learning smart decision-making."

❌ **Wrong:** "This will definitely go up!"
✅ **Right:** "Markets move one tick at a time."

## 🚫 BRAND GUARDRAILS (Non-Negotiable)

### NEVER
- ❌ Use hype language ("moon," "YOLO," "get rich")
- ❌ Make predictions ("this will go up," "buy now")
- ❌ Imply guaranteed returns
- ❌ Use neon colors or gradients in primary logo
- ❌ Make the mascot look like a trader
- ❌ Market to parents using the mascot
- ❌ Suggest gambling or speculation
- ❌ Use childish design elements
- ❌ Compromise on professional quality
- ❌ Confuse discipline with restriction

### ALWAYS
- ✅ Emphasize learning and understanding
- ✅ Show how markets actually work
- ✅ Celebrate effort over outcomes
- ✅ Use consistent, professional design
- ✅ Keep mascot as a guide, not a sales tool
- ✅ Lead with trust, introduce character later
- ✅ Maintain discipline across all touchpoints
- ✅ Explain concepts clearly
- ✅ Treat parents as intelligent decision-makers
- ✅ Treat kids as capable learners

## 🏆 THE BRAND SUCCESS METRIC

You'll know the brand is working when:

1. **Parents instantly feel safe**
   - Not because of cuteness, but because of professionalism

2. **Investors see discipline**
   - In every logo application, every color choice, every interaction

3. **Kids bond with Tick**
   - Without Tick feeling exploitative or salespeople-y

4. **Everyone understands the purpose**
   - Without needing explanation

5. **The brand can scale**
   - From app to schools to enterprise without losing identity

6. **Consistency is second nature**
   - Team automatically makes on-brand decisions

7. **The brand is defensible**
   - Clear principles guide every decision

**If all seven are true, you have a brand worth protecting.**
