# TickR Logo Usage Guidelines (v1.0)

## 1. Official Logo Assets

TickR has three approved logo formats. No others are permitted.

### A. Primary Logo (Default)
**Mascot + Wordmark**
- **File:** `PROJECT_DOCS/Brand_Assets/TickR_Primary_Logo_Dark.png`
- **Use for:** website headers, app store listings, pitch decks, marketing pages
- This is the flagship representation of the brand

### B. Mascot-Only Mark
**Robot + Arrow**
- **File:** `PROJECT_DOCS/Brand_Assets/TickR_Mascot_Mark_Dark.png`
- **Use for:** app icon, favicon, loading screens, avatars, badges
- This is the iconic symbol of TickR

### C. Wordmark-Only
**“TickR” text**
- **Files:** `PROJECT_DOCS/Brand_Assets/TickR_Wordmark_Dark.png` (Dark BG), `PROJECT_DOCS/Brand_Assets/TickR_Wordmark_Light.png` (Light BG)
- **Use when:** space is limited or a more serious tone is required
- **Common use cases:** documents, school materials, parent-facing PDFs

**Do not create new combinations.**

---

## 2. Color Rules (Non-Negotiable)

### Primary Color
- **TickR Blue** (approved brand blue only)

### Background Usage
- **Dark background:** use standard blue logo
- **Light background:** use approved light-background wordmark

### What’s Not Allowed
- ❌ Recoloring the logo
- ❌ Adding gradients
- ❌ Neon, rainbow, or seasonal colors
- ❌ “Just for fun” color variants

**Color variation is allowed ONLY for in-app mascot skins, never the core logo.**

---

## 3. Sizing & Spacing

### Minimum Size Rules
- **Primary Logo:** Never smaller than legible text
- **Mascot-Only:** Preferred for small sizes (favicons, app icons)

### Clear Space Rule
- Leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of the “T” in TickR
- No text, icons, or edges may enter this space

### Never:
- Stretch
- Compress
- Crop
- Rotate
- Skew
- Add drop shadows or glows

---

## 4. Background & Contrast

### Allowed:
- Solid black
- Solid white
- Neutral dark gray
- Clean brand-approved UI backgrounds

### Not allowed:
- Busy images
- Low-contrast backgrounds
- Patterns that reduce legibility

**If contrast is questionable, don’t use the logo—use text instead.**

---

## 5. Mascot Rules (Critical)

The mascot represents discipline, progress, and learning.

### Allowed:
- Using mascot as-is
- Using approved expression variants (happy, focused, learning)
- In-app reward skins (clearly separated from brand logo usage)

### Not allowed:
- ❌ Changing facial expressions in the logo
- ❌ Adding props, speech bubbles, emojis
- ❌ Turning mascot into a joke or meme
- ❌ Removing the arrow from the mascot

**Arrow = progress. It is sacred.**

---

## 6. What You Must Never Do

- ❌ Create alternate logos
- ❌ Modify typography
- ❌ Animate the logo in a hype-driven way
- ❌ Use the logo to imply financial returns
- ❌ Place the logo next to gambling, crypto, or “get rich” messaging

**TickR is training, not speculation. The logo must always reflect that.**

---

## 7. Tone Alignment

The TickR logo should always appear in contexts that feel:
- Structured
- Safe
- Educational
- Competitive (but calm)

**If a placement feels hypey, chaotic, or aggressive, don’t use the logo there.**

---

## 8. Final Rule (The One That Matters)

If a use case makes you ask:
“Is this pushing TickR toward hype instead of discipline?”
The answer is no—don’t do it.
