# TickR Logo System Specification

**⚠️ SUPERSEDED BY [Logo Usage Guidelines v1.0](./Logo_Usage_Guidelines_v1.0.md)**

**Verbal Design Spec for Clean System Logo (Primary Brand)**

This logo appears on investor decks, parent pages, app store, legal surfaces, and marketing headers.

---

## 🎯 GOAL

Look credible, modern, calm, and scalable — not hype-driven.

The logo must build immediate trust with parents and investors before they even read copy.

---

## 🔷 PRIMARY LOGO SYSTEM

### Core Mark

**Text-first wordmark: TickR**

- Capital T
- Capital R
- Rounded but confident typography
- No gimmicks, no glow
- Clean, understated

**Why text-first?**
Because TickR isn't a visual symbol—it's a product and philosophy. The name is the brand.

---

## 🎨 OPTIONAL ICON

**Purpose:** Scalable at small sizes (favicon, app icon, social profiles)

**Key Rule:** If the icon disappears, the wordmark still stands alone.

The wordmark must work perfectly without any icon.

### Icon Concept Options (Pick ONE)

#### Option 1: Upward Checkmark
- A checkmark that subtly angles upward
- Represents progression and completion
- Works at any size
- Suggests "checked off progress"

#### Option 2: Rising Bars
- A stylized "T" formed from two bars
- One horizontal (stability)
- One rising (progress)
- Minimal and geometric
- Suggests both structure and growth

#### Option 3: Subtle Bar Chart
- Minimal bar-chart where the last bar is slightly higher
- Evokes progress over time
- Market-relevant without being obvious
- Abstract enough to feel modern

**Recommendation:** Option 1 (upward checkmark) is cleanest and most scalable.

---

## 🎨 COLOR PALETTE (System-Safe)

### Primary Color
- Deep teal or slate blue
- RGB: Recommend #1B7A7D (teal) or #2C3E5F (slate blue)
- Conveys trust, stability, intelligence
- Professional without being corporate
- Works equally well on light and dark backgrounds

### Secondary Accent
- Soft green
- Used sparingly (only in celebrations, progress, unlocks)
- Never used in primary logo
- Represents growth and positivity
- RGB: Recommend #4CAF50 or #5FB85F (muted green)

### Neutrals
- White (backgrounds, clarity)
- Charcoal (deep dark for contrast)
- Light gray (secondary text)

### What NOT to Use
- ❌ Neon colors (hype-driven, untrusty)
- ❌ Glows or shadows on primary logo
- ❌ Gradients (will not scale properly)
- ❌ Multiple color variations of main logo

**Rule:** The primary logo is monochromatic or two-color max.

---

## 💫 FEEL & TONE

The logo should feel like:

✔ "This looks like something a smart parent trusts."

✔ "This could sit next to Duolingo or Khan Academy without feeling off."

✔ "This will still look good in 10 years."

---

## 📏 SCALING RULES (Non-Negotiable)

The logo must work perfectly at:

### 16px (Favicon)
- Icon is clear and readable
- Text is not small enough to be illegible
- Single color preferred
- No fine details lost

### Pure Black
- On white background
- Should look professional and clear
- No color dependence

### Pure White
- On dark background
- Should be visible and strong
- No color dependence

### Full Color
- On light backgrounds
- On dark backgrounds
- On photographic backgrounds

**Red Flag:** If it fails any of these tests, it's not done.

## 📐 LOGO VARIATIONS

### Horizontal Lockup (Primary)
```
[Icon] TickR
```
- Icon and text side-by-side
- Used on app store, headers, landing pages

### Stacked Lockup
```
[Icon]
TickR
```
- Icon above text
- Used in square contexts (app icons, avatars, social profiles)

### Icon Only
```
[Icon]
```
- Standalone icon
- Used at very small sizes (16px, 32px favicon)
- Only if icon is strong enough to work alone

### Text Only
```
TickR
```
- Wordmark alone
- Used when space is limited
- Always an option, never forced to use icon

## 💡 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Simplicity
- Clean, minimal lines
- No unnecessary complexity
- Readable at any size

### Timelessness
- Not trendy
- Not dated
- Will look current in 5+ years

### Versatility
- Works in all contexts
- Works in all color modes
- Works at all scales
- Works in all media (print, screen, embroidered, etc.)

### Trust
- Professional and approachable
- Not corporate or stuffy
- Not playful or childish
- Serious but not intimidating

---

## 🚫 WHAT NOT TO DO

❌ Don't add effects
- No drop shadows
- No glows
- No 3D effects
- No gradients in primary logo

❌ Don't use gradients
- Hard to scale
- Hard to print
- Dates the design quickly

❌ Don't add unnecessary elements
- Every element must serve a purpose
- If you can remove it, remove it

❌ Don't use hype-driven visual language
- No arrows (too startup-y)
- No rockets (too venture-y)
- No trendy shapes (will feel dated in 2 years)

❌ Don't include speech bubbles or chat elements
- This is not a social platform
- Avoid anything that suggests "community first"

## 📋 LOGO USAGE GUIDELINES

### Where to Use the Full Color Logo
- Brand website
- Marketing materials (slides, videos)
- Professional contexts

### Where to Use the Icon Only
- App store icon
- Favicon
- Social media profile picture
- Small spaces (<64px)

### Where to Use Text Only
- When icon is too small or illegible
- Legal documents
- Print where color is limited
- Email signatures

### Minimum Clear Space
- Logo should have clear space around it
- At least 1x the height of the "R" in all directions
- Nothing should touch or overlap the logo

### Minimum Size
- Never smaller than 128px wide on screen
- Never smaller than 0.5 inches wide in print
- Below these sizes, simplify or use icon only

## 🎯 SUCCESS METRICS

You'll know the logo is right when:

1. **Parents see it and instantly feel safe**
   - Not "this is for kids"
   - But "this is trustworthy education"

2. **Investors see it and think "serious"**
   - Not flashy
   - But definitely competent

3. **Kids see it and think "this is cool"**
   - Not patronizing
   - But definitely engaging

4. **It works everywhere**
   - App store, website, pitch deck, favicon, business card
   - Same quality and impact

5. **It won't need updating in 5 years**
   - Not trendy
   - Timeless but current

If all five are true, you have a great logo.
