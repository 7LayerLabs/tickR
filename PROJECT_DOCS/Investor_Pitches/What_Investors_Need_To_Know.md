# What to Tell an Investor (After They Read the Product Description)

## 1. The Core Business Insight

**The business isn't "a kids stock app." It's a retention engine disguised as education.**

Kids come for:
- Competition + real brands
- Progression and unlocks
- Social games with friends

Parents pay because:
- It's skill-building
- It's controlled
- It's safe

**That combo is rare:**
- Most edtech lacks stickiness
- Most "market games" lack trust

**TickR is built to have both.**

---

## 2. This Is a Wedge Into a Massive Lifelong Funnel

TickR is the on-ramp:
```
Ages 12–16 (TickR) → Teens → Young Adults → Real Investing Products
```

**Even if you never touch real trading**, you can monetize for years with:
- Subscription
- Tournaments
- Curriculum packs
- Family plans
- School licenses

**If you later integrate custodial account affiliates**, you've got a high-intent audience with parents already involved—and kids trained in disciplined behavior.

---

## 3. Your Moat Is "Real-Market Learning + Structured Competition + Safety"

Anyone can show stock prices.

Few can do:
- **Age-appropriate progression** (unlock metrics like P/E → PEG)
- **Scoring that punishes gambling behavior**
- **Safe community that parents tolerate**
- **A game loop that doesn't teach day-trading addiction**

That design choice is the defensibility.

You're not competing on data or features. You're competing on **system design that makes education addictive in a good way.**

---

## 4. The Key Insight: Competition Must Be Earned and Composite-Scored

Returns-only leaderboards produce YOLO behavior.

TickR's composite scoring (performance + diversification + consistency + learning) is the system that makes it:
- Educational while still addictive
- Rewarding while still disciplined
- Fun while still strategic

**That's the difference between "fun sim" and "training platform."**

---

## 5. Risk & Compliance Aren't Footnotes—Your Plan Actively Reduces Them

You're targeting minors and finance—investors will worry. **Address it directly:**

- No real money, no brokerage execution, no deposits/withdrawals
- Educational framing everywhere
- No "get rich," "beat the market," "returns" marketing language
- One daily portfolio snapshot to avoid day-trading loops
- Moderated, structured community (no DMs, no hype posts)

**This isn't regulatory theater. It's core design.**

---

## 6. What Success Looks Like (The KPIs You'll Run the Company On)

Investors want measurable traction. Tell them your core metrics:

### Activation
- % who buy their first stock within 10 minutes of onboarding

### Engagement
- Day-7 retention (the make-or-break for "game" products)
- Lessons completed per week (learning engagement)
- Portfolio check-ins per week (habit formation)

### Virality
- Friend invites per user
- Game creation rate
- Growth from network effects

### Monetization
- Paid conversion rate (free → $5.99/month)
- Churn rate (monthly)
- Lifetime value

### Safety
- Reports per 1,000 posts
- Moderator actions per 1,000 users
- Parent complaints (track and resolve)
