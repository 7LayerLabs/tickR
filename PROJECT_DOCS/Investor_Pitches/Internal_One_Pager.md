# TickR — Internal Summary One-Pager

**What partners email to each other after your meeting.**

---

## What It Is

TickR is a game-adjacent investing simulation platform for kids ages 12–16.

Users start with simulated cash, buy real stocks at real market prices, and manage a real-time portfolio—without any real money.

---

## Why It Matters

Most people learn investing through costly mistakes as adults.

TickR teaches market behavior, risk management, and emotional discipline before real money is involved.

---

## What's Different

Unlike stock games, TickR **structurally discourages gambling behavior**.

Performance is only part of the score.

**Diversification, consistency, and learning progress are weighted equally.**

This makes reckless strategies lose by design.

### The Scoring Formula

```
Final Score =
  50% Portfolio Performance +
  25% Risk & Diversification +
  15% Consistency (Green vs Red Days) +
  10% Learning Progress
```

A kid who YOLOs one stock will consistently lose to a kid who diversifies and learns.

## Engagement Model

- **Real brands** (Nike, Apple, Roblox) create daily real-world reinforcement
- **Daily portfolio changes** drive habit loops without intraday dopamine abuse
- **Friend competitions** create social gravity
- **Progression unlocks** (new sectors, metrics, tournaments) prevent boredom

---

## Risk Management (Not a Bug, It's Design)

**No real money.** Ever. No deposits, no withdrawals, no trading execution.

**No leverage, options, crypto, or penny stocks.** Hard-coded blocks.

**Parent dashboards & controls.** Full visibility, simple toggles.

**Structured, moderated community.** No DMs, no external links, no hype language.

**Educational framing throughout.** Never markets returns or "beat the market."

## Why Now

1. **Financial literacy is increasingly urgent**
   - 8+ states now mandate it
   - Parents want alternatives to TikTok finance advice
   - Gen Z is interested but untrained

2. **No good middle ground exists**
   - Edtech solutions are boring and low-retention
   - Stock simulators encourage gambling behavior
   - Brokerages won't serve minors
   - Finance social media is unmoderated hype

3. **TickR fills the gap**
   - Engaging like a game
   - Educational like Khan Academy
   - Safe like a parent-approved app
   - Behavioral like behavioral economics
