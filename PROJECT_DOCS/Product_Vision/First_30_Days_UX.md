# First 30 Days of User Experience

**This is where retention is won or lost.**

Most founders get this wrong. They build features and hope users string them together. You need a deliberate 30-day journey.

---

## DAY 0–1: INSTANT HOOK (First 10 Minutes)

### Goal
Get them to feel ownership immediately.

### What Must Happen

1. **Username created** (30 seconds)
   - No real names
   - 3–20 characters
   - Quick, fun

2. **Age band selected** (15 seconds)
   - 12–13, 14–15, 16
   - Language: "Who are you?"

3. **Starter sectors shown** (30 seconds)
   - Tech
   - Fashion & Lifestyle
   - Entertainment & Gaming
   - Everyday Brands
   - Language: "Start with brands you know"

4. **First stock purchased** (3–5 minutes)
   - See Apple, Nike, Netflix
   - Pick one, buy 5–10 shares
   - Watch portfolio value appear
   - Language: "You now own 5 shares of Nike"

### Why
If they don't own a real stock fast, they bounce. Period.

### Failure Mode to Avoid
"First lesson is about what stocks are." ← WRONG. They'll leave.

### Success Metric
65%+ of signups complete first purchase within 10 minutes.

---

## DAYS 2–5: FIRST EMOTIONAL RESPONSE

### Goal
Teach reaction, not panic.

### What Happens

1. **Portfolio changes** (daily)
   - Prices move based on real market
   - Green or red
   - No emotional language yet

2. **App asks what changed** (daily notification)
   - "Nike was up today. Why?"
   - 4 multiple-choice options:
     - Earnings
     - Competition
     - Trend
     - General market mood
   - No penalty for wrong answer
   - Explanation either way

3. **Points awarded** (first time)
   - Completing quiz = +10 points
   - Not about the answer, just for thinking

### The Critical Message
Red day ≠ failure
Red day = lesson

### Failure Mode to Avoid
- Celebrating green days, ignoring red days ← Creates hype culture
- Penalizing wrong answers ← Creates avoidance behavior
- Too much text ← They'll skip

### Success Metric
40%+ of users complete the "why did it move" quiz by Day 5.

---

## DAYS 6–10: SOCIAL GRAVITY

### Goal
Anchor the habit socially.

### What Unlocks

1. **Invite a friend button** appears
   - "Play with [Friend Name]?"
   - Creates or joins 7/30/60-day game
   - Game has its own leaderboard (friends only)

2. **First private game created or joined**
   - Same portfolio (now shared game)
   - Competition with visible scores
   - But only with people they know

3. **Leaderboard exposure** (first time)
   - Only their friends
   - Only composite scores, not holdings
   - Rank shown, "You're #2 of 3"

### Why This Works
Solo learning fades. Friendly rivalry sticks.

### Failure Mode to Avoid
- Making games paid-only ← Too much friction
- Showing other people's portfolios ← Privacy concern
- Global leaderboards now ← Not ready

### Success Metric
30%+ of users create or join a game by Day 10.

---

## DAYS 11–15: IDENTITY FORMATION

### Goal
Make them feel like "this is my thing."

### What Unlocks

1. **New sector unlocked** (first time)
   - Healthcare (or whichever was locked)
   - +10 new stocks to explore
   - Celebratory popup: "You unlocked Healthcare!"

2. **New metric visible** (P/E Ratio)
   - On stock pages now
   - "This stock costs $X for every $1 of earnings"
   - Simple explanation
   - Unlock comes with completing Level 2 lesson

3. **Portfolio diversification visual**
   - Pie chart of holdings
   - Sector breakdown
   - "You own X% of Tech, Y% of Healthcare..."
   - Visual goal: "Try to balance your sectors"

### Language Shift
From: "I'm playing a game"
To: "I'm building a portfolio"

### Failure Mode to Avoid
- Showing all metrics at once ← Overwhelming
- No explanation for P/E ← They'll ignore it
- Generic progression messages ← Feels robotic

### Success Metric
50%+ of users reach Level 2 by Day 15.

---

## DAYS 16–20: DEPTH WITHOUT FATIGUE

### Goal
Increase complexity without increasing effort.

### What Happens

1. **PEG Ratio unlocked** (with Level 3)
   - "P/E ratio divided by growth rate"
   - Why it matters: "High growth can justify high P/E"
   - Example: "Tesla's P/E is 50, but it's growing 50% annually"

2. **Strategy prompts appear** (contextual)
   - Kid owns only Tech
   - App: "Your portfolio is 90% Tech. That's concentrated risk. Want to try another sector?"
   - Not forced, just suggested
   - Language: "Here's a thought..."

3. **"Why did this outperform?" lessons**
   - Compare two stocks in same sector
   - "Nike outperformed Adidas this month. Why?"
   - Teaches competitive positioning
   - Quiz structure same as always

### Key
Still no walls of text. Ever.

### Failure Mode to Avoid
- Long explanations ← Use 2 sentences max
- Forcing diversification ← Make it suggested
- Too many new concepts ← One per 2 days

### Success Metric
40%+ of users balance portfolio across 2+ sectors by Day 20.

---

## DAYS 21–30: COMMITMENT POINT

### Goal
Convert free → paid or cement long-term usage.

### What Triggers Upgrade

1. **Want more sectors**
   - "You've unlocked 6 sectors. Ready for more?"
   - Paywall: "Upgrade for all sectors + more"

2. **Want games beyond basic**
   - "Create a tournament-style game"
   - Paywall: "30-day games with advanced rules"

3. **Want leaderboard access**
   - "See global leaderboard"
   - Paywall: "Compare to kids your age (opt-in)"

4. **Parent curiosity**
   - Parent sees kid talking about it
   - Kid: "Mom, can you see my portfolio?"
   - Parent dashboard: Paid feature
   - Paywall: "Full parent dashboard + insights"

### Parent Nudge (The Conversion Moment)

Weekly email to parent:
"This week, [Child] completed 2 lessons, earned 150 points, and diversified across 5 sectors. Here's what they learned: [bullet points]."

Link: "See full progress → Upgrade to $5.99/month"

### What Free Users Still Get

- Solo portfolio
- All 4 starter sectors
- Basic lessons
- Friend games (with limits)
- No leaderboards
- No parent dashboard

### What Paid Adds

- All 8 sectors
- Advanced lessons + metrics
- Unlimited friend games
- Leaderboards (friends + opt-in global)
- Parent dashboard
- Community access
- Tournament access (later)

### Pricing Moment Language
"You've learned a lot. Ready to go deeper?"

Not: "Unlock premium features"

### Success Metric
12–15% free-to-paid conversion by Day 30.

---

## THE CRITICAL PATH (Day 0–30)

```
D0–1: Ownership (first stock)
  ↓
D2–5: Emotion (red/green day)
  ↓
D6–10: Social (friend game)
  ↓
D11–15: Identity (new sectors)
  ↓
D16–20: Depth (strategy)
  ↓
D21–30: Commitment (paid or habit)
```

Each step depends on the previous one. Skip one and retention fails.

---

## RED FLAGS TO CATCH EARLY

### User Drops at D0–1
- Onboarding is too slow
- First stock purchase is not obvious
- They can't find familiar brands

Fix: Reduce friction. Fast.

### User Drops at D2–5
- No emotional resonance to portfolio changes
- Quiz feels like school
- No feedback on answers

Fix: Language shift. Make it feel conversational.

### User Drops at D6–10
- Games are hard to create or join
- Social feels awkward
- Leaderboard is confusing

Fix: Simplify social invitation. One tap to play.

### User Drops at D11–15
- Unlocks feel arbitrary
- P/E ratio confuses them
- No celebration for progress

Fix: Tighten explanations. Add unlock celebration.

### User Drops at D16–20
- Too many new concepts at once
- Strategy suggestions feel pushy
- Lessons are too long

Fix: Slow down. One concept every 2 days.

### User Drops at D21–30
- Paywall feels aggressive
- Parent email doesn't make sense
- Free tier feels incomplete

Fix: Shift language. Focus on depth, not gates.

## PARENT JOURNEY (Parallel to Child)

### Day 0–5
Parent hears about it from kid or sees kid using it.

### Day 6–10
Parent gets first weekly email with progress.

### Day 11–15
Parent notices kid checking app daily.

### Day 16–20
Parent asks: "Is this actually educational?"

### Day 21–30
Parent receives: "Here's what they learned" email
Parent thinks: "Worth $5.99/month for this?"

### Conversion Moment
Email with concrete evidence of learning → Dashboard invitation → Upgrade decision

---

## METRICS TO TRACK DAILY

### Activation Funnel
- Signups
- Username created (%)
- Sectors selected (%)
- First stock purchased (%)
- D1 return rate (%)

### Engagement Curve
- D1, D3, D7, D14, D30 retention
- Quiz completion rate
- Game creation rate
- Lesson completion rate

### Monetization
- Free → paid conversion
- Time to upgrade
- Parent conversion rate
- Churn (30-day)

### Behavioral Red Flags
- Concentration (% owning 1 stock only)
- Volatility (large daily moves)
- Trade frequency (indicating day-trading)
- Hype language in community

---

## THE HARDEST PART

**Nailing the emotional journey without overthinking it.**

Don't:
- Explain too much
- Celebrate too much
- Push too hard
- Lecture at all

Do:
- Show results
- Ask questions
- Let them discover
- Reward thinking

If it feels natural, you've got it right.
