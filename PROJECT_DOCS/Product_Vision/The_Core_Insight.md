## What Separates a "Kids Stock App" from TickR

### A "Kids Stock App" Does This:

- Shows real stock prices
- Lets kids buy stocks (simulated)
- Keeps score based on returns
- Has leaderboards
- Looks cool

**Outcome:** Kids learn that winning = having money. Results in YOLO behavior.

### TickR Does This:

- Shows real stock prices ✔
- Lets kids buy stocks (simulated) ✔
- Keeps score based on performance + diversification + consistency + learning
- Has leaderboards where pure returns can't win
- Looks cool AND teaches discipline

**Outcome:** Kids learn that winning = managing risk. Results in long-term thinking.

**Same data. Different system. Different behavior.**
