# Learning Levels (1–7)

## Level 1 — Companies & Brands
**What Kids Learn:**
- What companies are
- Products → revenue
- Why companies matter

**Unlocks:**
- Ability to buy stocks

**Lesson Format:**
- Interactive cards
- Real-world examples (Nike, Apple, Netflix)
- Quiz: "What does this company sell?"

---

## Level 2 — Stocks & Ownership
**What Kids Learn:**
- What stocks are
- What shares mean (ownership pieces)
- Price × shares = portfolio value
- Dividends (basic)

**Unlocks:**
- Portfolio value calculations
- Transaction history

**Lesson Format:**
- Visual: Pie chart of company ownership
- Interactive: "If Nike is worth $100 and you own 5 shares at $20 each..."

---

## Level 3 — Sectors
**What Kids Learn:**
- Why companies group together
- Industry patterns
- How sectors rise/fall together

**Unlocks:**
- Access to all sectors (from starter 4)
- Sector comparison tools
- Sector news feed

**Lesson Format:**
- Clickable sector map
- "Compare Tech vs Fashion" challenge

---

## Level 4 — Risk & Diversification
**What Kids Learn:**
- One stock vs many (concentration risk)
- Portfolio thinking (don't put all eggs in one basket)
- How diversification lowers risk

**Unlocks:**
- Diversification scoring on portfolio
- Risk badge/indicator

**Lesson Format:**
- Simulation: "10k all in Apple" vs "1k in 10 companies"
- Show volatility difference

---

## Level 5 — Valuation (P/E Ratio)
**What Kids Learn:**
- What P/E means (price per dollar of earnings)
- Expensive vs cheap
- Why overpaying hurts long-term returns

**Unlocks:**
- P/E ratio visible on stock pages
- P/E comparison tools

**Lesson Format:**
- "Stock A: P/E 50 (expensive) vs Stock B: P/E 12 (cheap)"
- Historical example: "Netflix P/E 100 in 2010, now 20"

---

## Level 6 — Growth + Valuation (PEG Ratio)
**What Kids Learn:**
- When high P/E makes sense (growing companies)
- PEG = P/E divided by growth rate
- Context matters

**Unlocks:**
- PEG ratio on stock pages
- Growth % YoY

**Lesson Format:**
- "Why Tesla's high P/E is justified (50% growth)"
- "Why Kodak's low P/E didn't help (declining)"

---

## Level 7 — Advanced Play
**What Kids Learn:**
- Advanced metrics (still simplified)
- Earnings reports & impact
- Sector rotation strategies

**Unlocks:**
- Ability to enter public tournaments
- Advanced competition modes
- Access to analyst summaries

**Lesson Format:**
- Real earnings examples
- "What happens when Apple reports earnings"
- Case studies of company pivots

---

## Progression Rules

- **No skipping levels**
- Earn through lessons, not cash
- Estimated time to Level 7: 4–8 weeks (casual play)
- Each level takes progressively longer
- Levels are persistent and account-wide

## Data Model

```
User.learning_level: 1-7
User.lessons_completed: [lesson_id]
User.unlocked_features: [feature_id]
```
