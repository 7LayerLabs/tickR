# Stock Page Template (Locked Template)

Every stock page follows this structure:

## Section 1: What This Company Does
- **Format:** One sentence max
- **Example:** "Nike designs and sells athletic shoes, apparel, and sports equipment"
- **Purpose:** Immediate clarity on business

## Section 2: Why People Know It
- Brand recognition
- Popular products
- Company culture
- **Format:** 2-3 bullet points, visual brand assets (logo, iconic product)

## Section 3: How It Makes Money
- Icons + short labels
- **Example:**
  - 👟 Shoe sales (40% of revenue)
  - 👕 Apparel (35%)
  - ⚽ Sports equipment (25%)
- **Purpose:** Demystify revenue streams

## Section 4: Sector World
- Show related companies in same sector
- Clickable → teaches industry context
- **Example:** "Other athletic brands: Adidas, Under Armour, Puma"

## Section 5: Key Stats (Unlocked Only)
Available only after kid unlocks via learning:
- **Always Visible:** Price, Daily change %
- **Unlocked at Level 3:** Market cap (simplified: "Worth $XX billion")
- **Unlocked at Level 5:** P/E ratio
- **Unlocked at Level 6:** Growth % (YoY)

## Section 6: Why Did the Price Move?
Interactive learning moment:
- **Format:** Multiple choice question
- **Options:**
  - Earnings report
  - Competition
  - Trend/news
  - General market mood
- **Reward:** Correct answer = points + explanation
- **Retry:** Wrong answer = explanation + try again

## Action Buttons

- **"Buy Shares"** → Opens purchase flow
- **"Sell Shares"** (if owned) → Opens sell flow
- **"Learn More"** → Links to relevant lesson

## Design Notes

- Clean, uncluttered layout
- Visual hierarchy: Sections, not walls of text
- Color coding: Green price up, red price down
- Responsive: Touch-friendly on mobile
