# Point System (FINAL)

## What Points Are For

Points are the progression currency:
- Unlock sectors
- Unlock metrics (P/E, PEG, growth %)
- Unlock competition modes
- Unlock tournaments

**Points are never used as money.** They don't buy stocks or increase portfolio value.

## How Points Are Earned

### Lesson Completion
- Complete Level 1 lesson: +50 points
- Complete Level 2 lesson: +75 points
- Complete Level 3–7 lessons: +100 points each

### Quiz Answers
- Correct answer: +10 points
- Incorrect answer: +0 (no penalty, just explanation)

### Reflection Questions
- Complete daily reflection: +15 points
- Complete weekly reflection: +25 points

### Portfolio Performance (Once Per Day)
- 🟢 Portfolio ends green: +25 points
- 🔴 Portfolio ends red: -5 points (small penalty, learning focus)
- Break-even: +0 points

**Philosophy:** Learning always outweighs market losses. We don't reward luck, we reward discipline.

## No Intraday Dopamine Abuse

- Points only calculate **once per day** (end of market)
- No real-time point pings
- No sound effects on point gains
- No leaderboard refreshes during market hours

This prevents:
- Constant refreshing
- Emotional decision-making
- Gambling-like behavior

## Example Point Earning

**Day 1:**
- Complete "Companies & Brands" lesson: +50 points
- Answer 2/3 quiz questions correctly: +20 points
- Portfolio ends green +$200: +25 points
- **Total: 95 points**

**Day 5:**
- Complete "Sectors" lesson: +100 points
- Answer reflection question: +15 points
- Portfolio ends red -$50: -5 points
- **Total: 110 points**

## Point Milestones

| Milestone | Unlock |
|-----------|--------|
| 100 points | All starter sectors (Tech, Fashion, Entertainment, Brands) |
| 250 points | Bonus sectors (Healthcare, Energy, Finance) |
| 500 points | P/E ratio on all stock pages |
| 750 points | PEG ratio + growth % |
| 1000+ points | Qualify for tournaments + advanced metrics |

## Display Strategy

- Show points subtly (top of screen, small counter)
- Show milestone progress (not a bar, just "750/1000")
- Show what you earned today (notification style, not aggressive)
- No celebration animations
