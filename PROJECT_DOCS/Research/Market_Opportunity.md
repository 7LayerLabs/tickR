# Market Opportunity & Competitive Analysis

## Market Size

### Target Demographic

- **US population ages 12–16:** ~21 million
- **Household income $50k+:** ~60% = 12.6 million
- **With parental interest in financial education:** ~5–10% = 630k–1.3M

### Market Context

**Gen Z Financial Interest:**
- 70% interested in investing (Gallup, 2022)
- 45% have heard of "day trading" (vs 20% millennials at same age)
- 30% have looked up stocks online
- 15% want to start investing

**Problem:** No safe, structured platform for this interest.

### TAM Estimation

**Conservative:**
- 630k potential users
- 8% penetration = 50k users
- ARPU $5/month = $3M+ ARR

**Optimistic:**
- 1.3M potential users
- 15% penetration = 195k users
- ARPU $6/month = $14M+ ARR

**TickR target (Year 3):** 75k users, $648k ARR

## Current Solutions & Gaps

### Educational Platforms

**Khan Academy / Investopedia**
- ✔ Trusted, comprehensive
- ❌ Boring, no real stocks, no gamification
- ❌ Not designed for hands-on learning

**Schools (Stock Market Simulation Clubs)**
- ✔ Free, organized
- ❌ Fragmented (no standard platform)
- ❌ Low engagement (not gamified)
- ❌ No network effects

**MarketWatch Game**
- ✔ Real data, real stocks
- ❌ Outdated (last updated 2015?)
- ❌ Clunky UI (looks like 2000s website)
- ❌ Zero engagement features

### Real Money Platforms

**Robinhood (has "Robinhood Snacks" for kids)**
- ✔ Real stocks, real money
- ❌ No educational curriculum
- ❌ Parent trust issues (framing as "financial empowerment")
- ❌ Risk of real losses

**Fidelity Youth Investing**
- ✔ Real stocks, custodial accounts
- ❌ Requires parent account
- ❌ No gamification
- ❌ Complex interface

### Social/Hype Platforms

**TikTok / Reddit / Discord**
- ✔ Viral, engaging, social
- ❌ Unmoderated, hype-focused
- ❌ No education
- ❌ Parents hate it (rightfully)

## Customer Segments

### Primary: Kids (12–16)

**Motivation:**
- Learn about money without risk
- Compete with friends
- Feel grown up / sophisticated
- Gain knowledge (second-order effect)

**Barriers:**
- Skepticism (is it boring?)
- Parental permission needed
- Time commitment concerns

### Secondary: Parents

**Motivation:**
- Financial education for kids
- Safer alternative to real investing / social media
- Parental visibility
- Peace of mind

**Barriers:**
- Skepticism (is it educational?)
- Regulatory concern (is it legal?)
- Cost (will pay if see value)

### Tertiary: Teachers / Educators

**Motivation:**
- Supplement curriculum
- Increase student engagement
- Save prep time

**Barriers:**
- Integration with curriculum
- Need teacher dashboard
- Free or very cheap
