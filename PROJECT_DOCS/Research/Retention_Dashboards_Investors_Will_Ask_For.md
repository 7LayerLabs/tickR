# Retention Dashboards: What Investors Will Ask For

**If you raise capital, someone will ask to see retention. Not vanity metrics. Real ones.**

Here's what you need and how to talk about them.

---

## 📊 CORE EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD (THE BIG ONE)

### 1. Activation

**Metric:**
% of new users who buy their first stock within 10 minutes

**Why investors care:**
This proves the hook works. It proves you can get from "download app" to "owns stock" without friction.

**Target for MVP:**
65–80%

**If you're at 40%:**
Your onboarding is too slow. Fix it before raising money.

**How you talk about it:**
"65% of signups have purchased their first stock within 10 minutes. This tells us the core loop is obvious and frictionless."

---

### 2. Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 30 Retention

**Metric:**
% of users returning on Day+1, Day+7, Day+30

**Why investors care:**
This tells them if TickR is a toy or a habit.

**Narrative:**
- D1 = Curiosity
- D7 = Habit formation
- D30 = Identity ("I'm an investor")

**Targets:**
- D1: 50%+ (curiosity hook works)
- D7: 30%+ (habit sticks)
- D30: 12%+ (becomes daily ritual)

**If you're at:**
- D1: 30% → Your hook is weak
- D7: 15% → Your progression feels grindy
- D30: 5% → You don't have product-market fit

**How you talk about it:**
"We're seeing 30% Day-7 retention, which indicates habit formation rather than novelty. By Day-30, 12% of cohorts remain active—comparable to successful games but with learning progression intact."

---

### 3. Portfolio Check-Ins per Week

**Metric:**
Average portfolio views per user per week

**Why it matters:**
TickR lives or dies on daily relevance. If kids aren't checking their portfolio weekly, the loop is broken.

**Target:**
3–5x per week per user (without spammy notifications)

**If you're at:**
- 1x/week → Kids aren't building habits
- 5x+/week → Good engagement
- 10x+/week → You're pushing notifications too hard (risky for parents)

**How you talk about it:**
"Users check their portfolio 4.2x per week on average, indicating daily habit formation. This is driven by real market changes, not push notifications."

---

### 4. Learning Engagement

**Metric:**
- Lessons completed per active user per week
- Quiz completion rate
- Concepts mastered

**Why investors care:**
Proves this isn't just a market watcher. Proves the educational loop works.

**Target:**
- 1–2 lessons per week per active user
- 80%+ quiz completion rate

**If you're at:**
- 0.3 lessons/week → Learning feels optional
- 50% quiz completion → Kids are skipping
- 0 concepts unlocked → No progression

**How you talk about it:**
"Active users complete 1.5 lessons per week, indicating that learning progression is intrinsic to engagement, not a separate activity."

---

### 5. Social Gravity

**Metric:**
- % of users who join or create a private game
- Average friends per user
- Game creation rate
- Game completion rate (finish full 7/30/60 days)

**Why it matters:**
This is organic retention and low CAC.

If kids are inviting friends, you have virality.

**Target:**
- 30%+ of users join or create a game
- Avg 2–3 friends per user who games
- 60%+ of games complete their full duration

**If you're at:**
- 10% games created → Social layer isn't sticky
- <1 friend avg → Not viral
- 30% game completion → Games feel incomplete or punishing

**How you talk about it:**
"30% of users create or join games, indicating strong social gravity. Average game has 3.2 participants and 65% complete the full duration."

---

### 6. Conversion Funnel (The Most Important)

**Metric:**
- Free → Paid conversion rate
- Time to upgrade (when measured from signup)
- Repeat customers (resub rate)

**Why investors care:**
Shows pricing power without pressure. Validates monetization isn't theoretical.

**Targets:**
- 12–15% free-to-paid within 30 days
- Median time to upgrade: 18–25 days
- 70%+ month-to-month retention

**If you're at:**
- <5% conversion → Willingness to pay doesn't exist
- >40 days median to upgrade → You're not capturing value
- <40% subscription retention → Pricing is wrong

**How you talk about it:**
"We're seeing 14% free-to-paid conversion within 30 days, with median upgrade at day 20. Month-to-month churn is 18%, indicating strong product-market fit for monetization."

---

### 7. Churn Reasons (Qualitative - CRITICAL)

**Metric:**
Exit surveys tagged into buckets

Categories:
- "Too boring"
- "Too hard"
- "Didn't understand"
- "Parent said no"
- "Lost interest"
- "Technical issues"

**Why it matters:**
Shows you understand why users leave. Signals where to focus fixes.

**Good signals:**
- "Parent said no" (means product worked, but family constraint)
- "Want real money" (means they're ready for next level)

**Bad signals:**
- "Too boring" (product design issue)
- "Don't understand" (onboarding issue)
- "Confusing" (UX issue)

**How you talk about it:**
"Of churning users, 35% cite parental controls (expected for minors), 25% want real trading (upgrade opportunity), 20% said 'too boring' (indicates engagement refinement opportunity), 20% technical issues (resolved in latest update)."

---

### 8. Safety & Trust Dashboard (VERY IMPORTANT)

**Metrics:**
- Community reports per 1,000 users
- Moderator actions per 1,000 posts
- Parent feature toggles usage (how many parents use controls)
- Complaint resolution time

**Why investors care:**
This is minors + finance. You must show control.

**Targets:**
- <2 reports per 1,000 users (healthy moderation)
- <1% of posts require removal (tight community)
- >50% of parents use at least one toggle (engaged parent base)
- <24hr resolution time on complaints

**If you're at:**
- >5 reports per 1,000 → Community is noisy or unsafe
- >5% posts removed → Moderation policy isn't working
- <30% parent toggles used → Parents don't feel they need control (good or bad?)
- >72hr response → You're not taking safety seriously

**How you talk about it:**
"We maintain <1% post removal rate through structured topic-based posting (not open feeds). Parent controls are toggled by 62% of parents, indicating high trust in our safety model."

---

## 📋 HOW TO PRESENT THESE DASHBOARDS

### In a Board Meeting
"Here's our core retention dashboard. The key findings: [3 most important metrics]. Where we're monitoring: [2 metrics we're improving]. Our biggest strength: [1 metric that proves defensibility]."

### In an Investor Call
Never show raw dashboards. Always tell the story first, then show evidence:

"Kids are forming daily habits around portfolio management. Here's proof: [activation rate], [retention curve]. The learning loop is working: [lessons/week]. And monetization is real: [conversion rate]. Let me walk you through each."

### In a Blog Post / Transparency Report
"We measure success by behavioral outcomes, not vanity metrics. Here's what we're tracking and why it matters..."

---

## 🎯 WHAT DASHBOARDS TO BUILD BEFORE YOU RAISE

**Minimum to raise seed capital:**
- [ ] Activation (first stock purchase within 10 min)
- [ ] D1/D7/D30 retention
- [ ] Portfolio check-ins per week
- [ ] Free → paid conversion rate
- [ ] Churn reasons (exit surveys)

**Nice-to-have but impressive:**
- [ ] Learning engagement (lessons/week)
- [ ] Social gravity (games created)
- [ ] Safety metrics (reports, removals)

---

## 💡 RED FLAGS TO ADDRESS PROACTIVELY

### If your D7 retention is <20%
Tell investors: "We're focused on [specific fix], expecting improvement to [target] by [date]."

Don't hide it. Address it.

### If your conversion is <8%
Tell investors: "We're validating monetization during beta. Early signs show [evidence of willingness to pay]. We'll have 12%+ conversion proof by [date]."

### If your moderation costs are high
Tell investors: "At scale, moderation is 1 person per 10k users, costing $X/user/year. This is fully absorbed into our margin."

---

## 🏆 THE BEST DASHBOARD

The best dashboard tells a story:

1. **Hook works** (activation rate)
2. **Habit forms** (D7 retention)
3. **Engagement sticks** (portfolio check-ins)
4. **Learning happens** (lessons completed)
5. **Friends drive retention** (games created)
6. **Parents trust it** (safety metrics)
7. **Monetization is real** (conversion rate)
8. **Users understand why to churn** (exit reasons)

If you can tell that story with data, you can raise capital.

---

## FINAL GUIDANCE

### Before You Raise
- [ ] Beta test with 100–200 users for 30+ days
- [ ] Collect clean retention data for at least 3 cohorts
- [ ] Build simple dashboards showing the story
- [ ] Validate 12%+ paid conversion (or admit it's aspirational and explain why)
- [ ] Document churn reasons

### During Fundraising
- [ ] Lead with retention story, not TAM
- [ ] Show 3–4 key metrics, not 20
- [ ] Acknowledge gaps honestly
- [ ] Explain how you'll improve each metric post-capital

### Post-Raise
- [ ] Report on these metrics monthly
- [ ] Show progress toward targets
- [ ] Adjust targets if data proves them wrong
- [ ] Never make up data; show real numbers even if rough

**Remember:** Investors don't expect perfection. They expect honesty about what's working, what isn't, and what you'll fix.
